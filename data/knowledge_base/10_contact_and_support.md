# NovaTech Contact & Customer Support Policy

## Support Channels & Availability
NovaTech offers multiple support channels based on your subscription tier and request type:

- **AI Support Agent**: Available 24/7/365 for instant policy questions, order lookups, and account status inquiries.
- **Email Support**: support@novatech-example.com (Mon–Fri, 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM EST).
- **Live Chat & Phone Support**: Available for Pro and Premium tier subscribers through the customer dashboard.

## Response Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
- **Premium Plan**: First response within 15 minutes; 24/7 dedicated support engineers.
- **Pro Plan**: First response within 4 hours during standard business operating hours.
- **Basic Plan / Free Tier**: First response within 24 to 48 business hours.

## Human Escalation Protocol
If our AI Support Agent is unable to resolve your inquiry, detects complex billing discrepancies, or if you explicitly request a human representative, the conversation is flagged with an Escalation ID (e.g., ESC-XXXXXX) and assigned to a tier-2 customer support specialist.
