# NovaTech Cancellation Policy

## Cancelling a Subscription
- You can cancel your NovaTech subscription at any time via your account portal or by contacting support.
- When you cancel your subscription, your account remains active with full tier benefits until the conclusion of the current prepaid billing period.
- No further charges will be billed to your payment method after cancellation.

## Data Retention After Subscription Cancellation
- Following subscription cancellation, your stored cloud data is preserved in read-only mode for 60 days to allow data export.
- After 60 days, inactive accounts without an active tier will have excess data permanently deleted according to our privacy and retention schedule.

## Reactivation
- You may reactivate a cancelled subscription at any time prior to account deletion by selecting a plan and updating your payment details.
