# NovaTech Accepted Payment Methods & Billing

## Supported Payment Options
NovaTech accepts the following payment methods across all store purchases and subscription billings:

- **Credit and Debit Cards**: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Discover.
- **Digital Wallets**: Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal.
- **Enterprise Invoicing**: Available exclusively for Premium and Enterprise business accounts with Net-30 payment terms upon credit approval.

## Billing Cycles
- Subscriptions are billed on a recurring basis (monthly or annually) on the calendar day corresponding to the initial activation date.
- All pricing is processed in USD ($) unless local currency pricing is explicitly enabled during checkout.

## Updating Payment Details
You can update your stored credit card or billing address at any time in Account Settings under Billing & Invoices. If a payment fails, our billing system will retry up to 3 times across 7 days before suspending active tier features.
