# NovaTech Subscription Renewal & Billing Cycles

## Automatic Renewal
- All NovaTech subscription plans (Basic, Pro, Premium) are set to automatically renew at the end of their respective billing cycle (monthly or annually).
- Renewal charges are billed to the primary payment method on file on the exact renewal date.

## Renewal Notices
- For annual plans, an email reminder is sent 30 days and 7 days prior to the scheduled renewal date detailing the upcoming charge amount and plan details.
- Monthly subscriptions do not receive monthly advance email notices, but invoice receipts are emailed immediately upon successful payment.

## Grace Period and Failed Renewals
- If a renewal payment fails (e.g., due to an expired card or insufficient funds), your account enters a 7-day grace period.
- During the grace period, all tier features remain accessible while our automated billing system retries the charge.
- If the payment is not resolved by the end of the 7-day grace period, the account is transitioned to "Suspended" status until valid payment details are provided.
