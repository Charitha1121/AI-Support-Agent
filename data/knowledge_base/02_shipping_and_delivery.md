# NovaTech Shipping and Delivery Times

## Shipping Options and Estimated Delivery
NovaTech ships to addresses across the United States, Canada, and select international destinations.

- **Standard Shipping**: 3 to 5 business days. Free for orders over $50 or for NovaTech Pro and Premium members.
- **Express Shipping**: 1 to 2 business days. Available for an additional $15 flat rate.
- **Overnight Priority**: Next business day delivery if ordered before 1:00 PM EST. Flat rate of $25.
- **International Shipping**: 7 to 14 business days depending on customs clearance.

## Order Processing Times
- Orders placed on business days before 2:00 PM EST are typically processed and handed over to our carrier on the same day.
- Orders placed over weekends or national holidays are processed on the next business day.

## Tracking Your Shipment
Once an order is handed to the shipping carrier, an automated tracking number is generated and emailed to the account holder. You can check the live tracking status at any time by asking our support system with your Order ID.
