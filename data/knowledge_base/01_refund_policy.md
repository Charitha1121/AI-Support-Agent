# NovaTech Refund Policy

## Eligibility for Refunds
NovaTech offers a 14-day refund policy for all hardware products, accessories, and digital subscription services from the original date of purchase or charge.

## Hardware & Physical Orders
- To be eligible for a full refund on physical items, the item must be unused, in its original packaging, and in the same condition that you received it.
- Items damaged due to customer misuse or missing accessories are not eligible for a full refund.
- Shipping fees incurred during original delivery are non-refundable unless the return is due to a NovaTech error or a defective item.

## Software & SaaS Subscriptions
- Monthly and annual subscription plans can be refunded within 14 days of the initial subscription activation or annual renewal date.
- Pro-rated refunds are not issued for monthly subscriptions requested after the 14-day grace period.

## Processing Timelines
- Once your returned item or refund request is received and inspected, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund.
- If approved, refunds are processed back to the original method of payment within 5 to 10 business days.
