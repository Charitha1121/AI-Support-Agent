# NovaTech Account Security & Authentication

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
- NovaTech strongly recommends enabling Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) on all user accounts.
- Supported 2FA methods include Authenticator Apps (TOTP via Google Authenticator, Authy, or 1Password) and SMS verification codes.
- Backup recovery codes are generated upon 2FA configuration. Store these codes securely.

## Password Requirements
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters in length and include uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and at least one special character.
- Passwords expire every 180 days for enterprise accounts.

## Suspicious Activity and Account Lockouts
- After 5 consecutive failed login attempts, an account is temporarily locked for 30 minutes.
- If you notice unauthorized access or suspicious activity, immediately trigger a password reset and notify our security team. Our automated support agent can escalate security incidents immediately to a human engineer.
