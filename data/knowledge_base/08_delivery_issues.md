# NovaTech Delivery Problems & Lost Shipments

## Delayed Shipments
- If your estimated delivery date (ETA) has passed and your package has not arrived within 48 hours of the projected date, please check your live tracking status or contact support.
- Severe weather events and carrier transit delays can occasionally impact delivery timelines.

## Damaged Packages Upon Arrival
- If your item arrives visibly damaged or the packaging is torn open, please take clear photos of the packaging and defective item within 48 hours of delivery.
- Contact support with your Order ID and photo attachments to receive an immediate replacement shipment at no additional cost.

## Missing or Stolen Packages ("Marked as Delivered")
- If your tracking status shows "Delivered" but you have not received the package:
  1. Check around your delivery area, porch, back doors, and with neighbors or building managers.
  2. Wait 24 hours, as carriers occasionally mark items delivered just prior to drop-off.
  3. If still missing, notify our support agent with your Order ID. We will file a carrier tracer investigation and issue a replacement or full refund.
