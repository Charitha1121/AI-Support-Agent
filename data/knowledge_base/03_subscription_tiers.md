# NovaTech Subscription Tiers & Pricing

NovaTech offers three subscription tiers designed to support individual users and scaling enterprises:

## Basic Plan ($9/month or $90/year)
- Core cloud storage (50 GB)
- Standard support via email (24-48 hour response SLA)
- Access to web applications
- Standard shipping rates on physical orders

## Pro Plan ($29/month or $290/year)
- Expanded cloud storage (500 GB)
- Priority support (under 4 hour response SLA)
- Free Standard Shipping on all hardware purchases
- Advanced analytics and multi-device sync
- 10% discount on accessory purchases

## Premium Plan ($79/month or $790/year)
- Unlimited cloud storage
- 24/7 dedicated VIP phone and chat support
- Free Express Shipping on all hardware orders
- Full enterprise collaboration tools and custom integrations
- Extended 2-year warranty on all purchased NovaTech hardware

## Changing Your Plan
You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time through your Account Settings. Upgrades take effect immediately with pro-rated billing, while downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
