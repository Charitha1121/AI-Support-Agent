# NovaTech Order Cancellation Policy

## Cancelling an Order Before Shipment
- You can request order cancellation as long as your order status is in "Processing" and has not yet been marked as "Shipped".
- To cancel an order, check your order status using your Order ID. If the order is eligible, our team or support agent can process the cancellation immediately.
- Once cancelled, an automatic 100% refund is initiated to your original payment method.

## Orders Already Shipped
- If an order has already transitioned to "Shipped" or "Out for delivery", it cannot be cancelled or intercepted in transit.
- Once you receive the package, you can initiate a standard return under our 14-day Refund Policy by requesting a prepaid return shipping label.

## Custom and Made-to-Order Items
- Custom-configured hardware rigs or specialized enterprise hardware cannot be cancelled once assembly has begun (typically 2 hours after order placement).
